03 / SAVINGS, CREDIT & MEMBER DEVELOPMENT

Save together.
Grow together.

Discover 7 Power SACCO is a proposed member-based initiative designed to promote a savings culture, responsible borrowing and shared prosperity.

01

Membership

A community of members united by shared financial goals, clear membership criteria and participation in governance.

02

Savings & credit

A future framework for regular member savings and fair, responsible credit facilities, subject to registration and all required approvals.

03

Financial empowerment

Financial literacy, investment education, member welfare and support for sustainable economic opportunities.

Important development notice

This SACCO is presented as a proposed initiative under development. It is not represented as registered, licensed or currently accepting deposits, savings, investments or loan applications. Operations and financial services will begin only after the appropriate registration, governance arrangements and regulatory approvals in Kenya have been completed.
